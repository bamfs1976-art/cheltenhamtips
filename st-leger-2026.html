Doncaster — the course

Left-handed, flat and galloping, and one of the fairest tracks in Britain. Doncaster is a wide, level, pear-shaped circuit of about 1m7½f with a long home straight of roughly four and a half furlongs. There is also a straight mile, used for the May Hill and the mile nurseries.

It rewards the best horse rather than the best-positioned one. There are no tight bends, no camber tricks and no short run-in, which is why it has staged the St Leger since 1776 — a genuine test of a stayer over 1m6f115y, and the same trip as the Park Hill.

A declared card carries the final field, a draw for every runner, jockeys for all of them, and the Betting Forecast that Step 5b needs to call STEAM or DRIFT. Declarations confirm 48 hours out: Tuesday 8 September for Thursday.

2 · Each-way terms off the bookmaker, not the racecard. The penalty values printed here are racecourse prize money — the Weatherbys pays down to 10th, which is not ten each-way places. With six races entered at 16+, the place band is the thing to read carefully, because it is what opens or shuts the LONG.

3 · External columns, once they publish. Mullington, Nick Luck, Spotlight, Raceolly, Grimshaw and the HRN big-priced lists get overlaid as badges — with horseracing.net counted for its prices and not its picks, since its free tips are Spotlight's own.
