Newcastle (Gosforth Park) — the course

Left-handed Tapeta (all-weather). Newcastle switched its round course and straight mile to Tapeta in 2016, so the Plate — once a stamina test on testing turf — is now run on a consistent synthetic surface. Galloping, fair, with a stiff uphill finish. The round course is a galloping ~1m6f circuit; the Plate is two full circuits-worth at 2 miles.

Draw & pace. On the straight-mile sprints the draw and pace map matter — Tapeta tends to ride fair but a slight edge to those who can lie up out of a low-to-middle draw over 5f–7f. Over the Plate trip (2m) the draw washes out; stamina, a good tactical position and proven Tapeta form are the keys. Draw analysis is still mandatory on every flat pick per the display contract.

Angles. The Plate rewards progressive, well-handicapped stayers dropping in or improving 3yos getting weight — Mark Johnston/Charlie Johnston and the northern yards have a strong record. Proven course-and-distance Tapeta form is a reliable signal; many synthetic specialists repeat-run well here. Going is a non-factor on the all-weather, but check the weather for the supporting turf hurdle/chase fixtures if any cross-code card applies.
