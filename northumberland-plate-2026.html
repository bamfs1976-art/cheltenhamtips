Newcastle (Gosforth Park) — the course

Left-handed Tapeta (all-weather). Newcastle switched its round course and straight mile to Tapeta in 2016, so the whole card is run on a consistent synthetic surface — galloping, fair, with a stiff uphill finish. Going is a non-factor; form on the surface and at the track is the key.

Draw & pace. On the round-course mile-plus (Scarborough 1m2f, Kippax 1m, the 18:07) the draw washes out and a good tactical position matters most. On the 5f Allerdene a low-to-middle berth can save a length — worth a small weighting. Draw analysis is still shown on every flat pick per the display contract.

Angles. Tim Easterby (David Allan) and the northern yards have a strong Newcastle record; Haggas, the Gosdens, Beckett and Charlie Johnston ship runners up here when well-handicapped. Proven course-and-distance Tapeta form repeats — many synthetic specialists run their race here every time. The festival builds to the Northumberland Plate (2m Heritage Handicap, "the Pitmen’s Derby") on Plate Day, Saturday 27 June.
