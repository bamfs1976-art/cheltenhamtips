How It Works

01
Expert Tips
Each festival gets NAP, NB and each-way picks from Racing Post tipsters, William Hill, HRN and specialist ante-post sources.
02
Live Results
Results entered race by race. Winners, SPs and placings captured and scored automatically in the tipster leaderboard.
03
Tipster Scoring
Win = 3 pts, Placed = 1 pt. The all-time league table grows with every completed festival throughout the year.
04
My Tracker
Add selections to your tracker inside each festival app. Picks saved in your browser so you can review them on the day.
