Ffos Las — the course

Left-handed. Galloping, fair track in the Gwendraeth valley, Carmarthenshire — opened 2009, the first new British racecourse for 80 years. Short run-in. No draw bias (it's a jumps track — draw is irrelevant; focus on going, trip, jumping and trainer/jockey form).

Going. Can ride soft to heavy through the winter — ground is the primary factor. Jockeys race up the middle on soft ground. Check the going and the forecast before any selection.

Key trainers (course-and-distance form patterns): Peter Bowen ~15% strike · Rebecca Curtis ~21% · David Pipe (LSP +15.42) · Venetia Williams (EW LSP +21.17) · plus the Welsh-yard regulars who know the track. Key jockeys: Sean Bowen ~20% · Adam Wedge ~19% · Gavin Sheehan ~28%. Trainer LSP at the course is a strong signal here.
