Epsom Downs · the camber, the Tattenham descent and the run-in

Left-handed · undulating · uniquely demanding. A 1m4f U-shape with no other British track like it. The early uphill section to the highest point, then a sharp downhill swing past Tattenham Corner, the back-straight pronounced lateral camber that throws unbalanced horses across the field, and a short ~3½f run-in. Horses who race straight, balanced and tactically beat gallopers who lean or pull.

Draw bias — Derby trip. Low draws (1–5) hold a modest historical edge in the Derby and Oaks — they save ground round Tattenham Corner. In the Dash sprint (5f 6y, Heritage Handicap, 20+ runners typical), draw is significant: high draws on the stand-side rail or middle group historically outperform low draws on soft ground; on good ground the bias flattens. Always check the going first.

Key trainer angles. Aidan O'Brien dominates the Derby in the modern era (8 wins) — any Coolmore runner with Ryan Moore demands respect; the second string under Wayne Lordan / Sean Levey often beats the market. Charlie Appleby (Adayar 2021) and J. & T. Gosden (Golden Horn 2015) the other Group-1 producers. William Haggas, Andrew Balding and Roger Varian have all targeted the Coronation Cup successfully with their best older middle-distance horses.

Key jockey angles. Ryan Moore, William Buick, Oisin Murphy, Tom Marquand and Colin Keane — the riders to follow at Epsom on Derby Day. Frankie Dettori's legacy run (2007 win on Authorized) sets the standard; the Tattenham Corner positioning move is what separates the great Epsom rides.

Going. Typically Good in early June; the watering team manage closely. A Good to Firm Derby is more orthodox; Good to Soft puts a real premium on stamina and reshapes the market. Forecast watch: SE England rain Thu / Fri evening is the key variable.

The Derby itself (Sat 15:30, G1, 1m4f, £1.5m to winner) is the most valuable flat race in Britain. ITV cover it as the centrepiece of their flat racing year. Watch the Saturday-morning weather and the going report closely before the engine's final NAP / NB / Longshot lock in.
