Newmarket (July Course) — the course

Right-handed into the straight, essentially a long galloping test. The July Course shares Newmarket’s wide, stiff, uphill character — a track that rewards genuine class and stamina and punishes the short-of-run. The round-course routes (Bahrain Trophy 1m5f, Princess of Wales’s 1m4f) are proper staying tests; the straight-course sprints and the mile handicaps put a premium on a good position and a strong finish.

Draw & pace. On the straight 6f a marginal edge can go to one side depending on where the pace lies, but the July Course is rarely a draw graveyard — it’s seldom decisive. Draw analysis is still shown on every flat pick per the display contract; only the big straight-course handicaps are worth any weighting.

Angles. Godolphin/Appleby, the Gosdens, Haggas and Balding dominate the Group and black-type races; the July Course honours-board is a class-horse’s track. Proven form at a mile-plus on a galloping surface is the reliable read. The festival builds to the July Cup (G1), the mid-season six-furlong sprint championship, on Saturday.
