The Roodee — Britain's Tightest Track

Chester is unique. A near-circular left-handed track measuring just over a mile in circumference, the Roodee is the smallest and tightest course in Britain. Speed and a low draw are everything — over a mile or less, single-figure stalls win the overwhelming majority of races. From 1m2f and beyond, the bias eases but rarely disappears.

Get a position from the gates, race close to the rail, and conserve enough to kick out of the home turn. Front-runners and prominent racers thrive; deep closers are penalised by the geometry of the track.

The Headline Races

Cheshire Oaks (Wed 14:35, Listed, 1m 3½f) — Key Epsom Oaks trial for fillies. Class fillies tested over middle distances at this trip.

Boodles Chester Vase Stakes (Wed 15:05, G3, 1m 4½f) — Traditional Derby trial. Aidan O'Brien has a phenomenal record. Look for a stoutly-bred 3yo who can settle and quicken.

Boodles Raindance Dee Stakes (Thu 14:35, Listed, 1m 2f 70y) — Three-year-old colts' Derby trial. Listed-class colts on a tight, tactical 1m2f.

Ladbrokes Ormonde Stakes (Thu 15:05, G3, 1m 5f 84y) — Older horses' middle-distance Group race. Winner gets an automatic berth in the Belmont Gold Cup at Saratoga.

Boodles Huxley Stakes (Fri 14:35, G2, 1m 2f 70y) — Upgraded to Group 2 in 2026. Manhattan Stakes Belmont ticket on offer for the winner. Pace and a low draw essential.

Ladbrokes Chester Cup (Fri 15:05, Heritage Hcap, 2m 2f 75y) — The Festival's flagship handicap. Stamina, course form and a workable mark are the trends. £87,618 to the winner.
