About the Festival

Opening Day — Thursday
Four Grade 1s including the Manifesto Novices' Chase and William Hill Aintree Hurdle. Often considered the best day for pure racing quality. Gates open 11am.
Ladies Day — Friday
The Melling Chase (Grade 1) and Topham Handicap Chase over the National fences. Famous for fashion — the Style Award is a major draw alongside top-class racing.
Grand National Day — Saturday
Seven races culminating in the 4pm Grand National itself. The Liverpool Hurdle (Grade 1) and Mersey Novices' Hurdle are supporting highlights. Gates open 10am.
Getting There
Aintree Racecourse, L9 5AS — 1 mile from the M57/M58. Merseyrail Aintree station is a 5-min walk. Car parks must be booked in advance and open at 6am.

The Grand National Fences

The National is run over two circuits — fences 1–16, Valentine's, then the Chair, Water Jump, then back around. 30 fences in total, 16 unique.

    Betting Guide

    Each-Way Betting
    Most bookmakers pay 5 places (1/4 odds) for the Grand National. With 34 runners, this represents excellent value — an each-way bet means you're paid if your horse finishes top 5.
    Non-Runner Money Back
    Many bookmakers offer NRMB for the National — if your selection is a non-runner, your stake is returned. Check individual bookie terms before placing ante-post bets.
    Best Odds Guaranteed
    BOG is widely offered on the National. Place your bet at current odds and if the SP is bigger, you're paid the SP. Place bets on the morning of the race for best value.
    Trends to Note
    8–9 year olds dominate. Average winning SP ~20/1. Favourites rarely win — only 6 in the last 25 years. Irish-trained horses have won 6 of the last 7. Weight under 11st is preferred.
