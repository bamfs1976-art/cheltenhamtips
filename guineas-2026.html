The Rowley Mile

Newmarket's Rowley Mile is the home of British Classic racing. A wide, straight, right-handed track running into the Dip before rising sharply to the finish. The straight mile is unforgiving — there is nowhere to hide, no bends to shelter behind, and pace is relentless from the off.

The camber favours those drawn in higher stalls for the mile Classics. Big-field, fast-ground races at the Rowley Mile have historically rewarded high draws — this is a significant factor in the Guineas and must be considered when assessing each-way chances.

2000 Guineas — Race Trends

The 2000 Guineas is the first Classic of the flat season for 3yo colts (and fillies can run). Run over one straight mile, it rewards sharp, high-class horses with natural pace rather than out-and-out stayers.

Last ran in a Classic trial — winners almost always ran in either the Craven, Greenham or Dewhurst
High draw — stalls 8+ have a strong advantage in fields over 10 runners
Racing Post Rating 115+ as a juvenile is a reliable qualifier
1 or 2 runs at 3 before the Guineas — too many runs a flag
Irish-trained horses — Aidan O'Brien has dominated in recent decades
Unbeaten juveniles — often trade short but sometimes find the step up too much
Single-figure odds winners — the Guineas regularly produces a 12/1+ winner
Heavy or soft going — alters the form book significantly; pace horses can struggle

1000 Guineas — Race Trends

The 1000 Guineas is restricted to 3yo fillies and is run on the same straight mile as the 2000 Guineas. It is Ireland's and France's first opportunity to claim a British Classic, and Irish-trained fillies frequently dominate.

Fillies' Classic trial — the Nell Gwyn (7f, Newmarket) and the Fred Darling (7f, Newbury) are the key prep races
Aidan O'Brien has trained more 1000 Guineas winners than any other trainer in recent history
High draw holds — same camber advantage as the 2000 Guineas
Previous Group 1 win as a juvenile strongly preferred
2yo RPR of 110+ is the typical benchmark for a winner
Unbeaten favourites — several have been beaten at short prices

Festival Overview

The Qipco Guineas Festival spans three days at Newmarket's Rowley Mile in early May. It is the centrepiece of British Classic season, with Friday's supporting card typically featuring key trials and Group races, Saturday building to the 2000 Guineas, and Sunday closing with the 1000 Guineas.

Prize money for each Classic is typically in excess of £500,000 — attracting the best 3yo talent from Britain, Ireland and France. The festival also features Group 2 and Group 3 races including the Dahlia Stakes, Palace House Stakes and the Abernant Stakes.
